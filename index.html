Questions and Answers

What are Twitch global badges?

Twitch global badges are chat icons displayed next to usernames across Twitch. Unlike channel subscriber or bit badges, global badges use the same official artwork everywhere.

Where do the user counts come from?

Ownership counts and percentages are synced from the public PotatBotat badge dataset and Twitch’s live GraphQL API.

How often are new badges tracked?

Automated snapshots sync every 2 hours via GitHub Actions, and your browser also compares live against Twitch on load.
